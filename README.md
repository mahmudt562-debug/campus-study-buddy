# Campus Study Buddy

## Team Member
Tanvir Ahmed

## Project Abstract
Campus Study Buddy is an application designed to help college students organize their academic work in one place. Students will be able to add their courses, assignments, quizzes, exams, and other academic tasks, keep track of upcoming deadlines, and mark completed work. The goal of the application is to provide a simple and user-friendly way for students to stay organized and manage their academic workload.
